Jordan Miles has spent 8 years living full-time in an RV across all 50 states. What started as casual road-tripping became a deep dive into the economics of RV living — particularly fuel costs, which she found make or break most budgets more than any other expense. With an automotive maintenance background, she tracks real-world fuel economy across every RV class and towing configuration. At RV Life Guide, Jordan covers trip cost planning, fuel efficiency strategies, and how to calculate realistic travel budgets before you hit the road.
