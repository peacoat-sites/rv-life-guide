Tony Reeves spent 15 years as an auto mechanic before transitioning to full-time RV work, and the skills transferred more than he expected. He has repaired everything from slide-out motor failures to water damage remediation, and believes every RV owner should understand the basics of their rig. At RV Life Guide, he covers preventive maintenance, DIY repair guides, and knowing when to call a professional.
